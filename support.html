Help and support

How do I drive?

Hold your device sideways. Put your left thumb down anywhere on the left and slide it to steer. GAS and BRAKE are on the right (hold BRAKE to back up). Going fast, steer and hold DRIFT to slide round bends, then let go for a boost. Prefer tilting? Switch on Tilt to steer in Settings. Want it easier? Switch on Easy drive and the gas is automatic.

What does the ABILITY button do?

Every kart has its own special ability, like a Honey Shield, a Dino Stomp or a Rainbow Dash. The garage says what each one does. A ring fills up while it recharges, and the button pops when it's ready.

How do I race my friends?

Tap RACE FRIENDS. There you can:

  • Race online: invite up to 3 friends and race together.
  • Challenge a friend: send your ghost to a friend. They race it when they like, and the faster time wins.
  • Race your best: race the ghost of your own fastest run on a track.

To race friends online or challenge them, you'll need to be signed in to Game Center (in the Settings app) and be Game Center friends with them. Everyone needs Chaos Karts on their own iPhone or iPad.

RACE FRIENDS says Game Center isn't available

Check you're signed in to Game Center in the Settings app and connected to the internet. If Screen Time limits multiplayer games, a parent can change that in Settings, under Screen Time, Content & Privacy Restrictions, then Game Center.

How do I earn coins?

Just race! You get coins for every race, and more for finishing higher up. Spend them in the garage on new karts, paint jobs and trails. There's nothing to buy with real money.
