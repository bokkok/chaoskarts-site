Privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

The short version: Chaos Karts doesn't collect anything about you. There are no ads, no tracking, no analytics, no accounts to make and nothing to buy.

What stays on your device

Your coins, the karts you've unlocked, your settings and your best times are saved on your iPhone or iPad. So is a recording of your fastest run on each track (your "ghost"). None of it is sent to us. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Racing friends with Game Center

Racing friends is optional, and uses Apple's Game Center. If you choose to sign in to Game Center:

  • Online races are with friends you invite (never strangers). While you race, the game sends what's needed for the race, like where your kart is, which buttons you press and which kart you picked, to your friends' devices through Game Center.
  • Ghost challenges send your recorded run (where your kart drove, and your time) to the friend you pick. Game Center keeps it until the challenge is over. Your friend sees your Game Center nickname.
  • There's no chat. The only messages are the game's own, like "sent you a ghost to race".

We never see or keep any of this. Game Center is run by Apple, and Apple's privacy policy covers it. You can sign out of Game Center in the Settings app.

Children

Chaos Karts is made for everyone, including children. We don't collect personal information from anyone, of any age. Parents can turn off multiplayer games and adding friends with Screen Time, in the Settings app under Content & Privacy Restrictions, then Game Center.

Changes
